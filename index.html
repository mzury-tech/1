Why it works

Matches your exact setup

The steps use real menu names for PS5, Xbox and PC, plus brand notes for LG, Samsung, Sony, ASUS and BenQ.

Honest about bandwidth

4K at 120 Hz and VRR need HDMI 2.1. On HDMI 2.0 those steps are flagged instead of hidden, so you know why.

Runs in your browser

The test cards are drawn on an HTML5 canvas. The motion card measures your screen’s real refresh rate.

Menu names vary by model and firmware year, so treat presets as a starting point. The white clipping card is SDR. For true HDR use PS5 Adjust HDR, the Xbox Calibration app or Windows HDR Calibration.
